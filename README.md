###### SIDDHARTHA &nbsp;·&nbsp; INTERACTIVE ARTWORK &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026 &nbsp;·&nbsp; MEASURED

An artwork about a person, and the source of every drawing on this page. The name is five sheets of light at five depths; from exactly one point they add up to a word. Keep still and it resolves. Move and it scatters. One continuous world in four parts, and it can be pulled out of its own window.<br>
<sub><i>HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with three.js vendored for three moments. No framework, build step, font files or network requests. Runs from a double-click.</i> &nbsp;→&nbsp; <a href="https://github.com/Siddhuperuri/miracle">Siddhuperuri/miracle</a></sub>

###### ORBIT &nbsp;·&nbsp; KNOWLEDGE AND DOCUMENT PLATFORM &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026 &nbsp;·&nbsp; ARRANGED

Upload documents into a workspace and ask questions answered from their contents, cited to the exact passage. Identity, workspaces, upload and the asynchronous pipeline are built; retrieval and chat are not, so they are drawn dashed. A modular monolith whose layering fails the build if it is broken, with 24 decision records for what was chosen and what was rejected.<br>
<sub><i>Python, FastAPI, Celery · Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind · PostgreSQL with pgvector, Redis, MinIO · Docker, GitHub Actions</i> &nbsp;→&nbsp; <a href="https://github.com/Siddhuperuri/orbit">Siddhuperuri/orbit</a></sub>

###### HELIOS &nbsp;·&nbsp; SOLAR ENERGY INTELLIGENCE &nbsp;·&nbsp; 2026 &nbsp;·&nbsp; INFERRED

Physics and machine learning for solar forecasting, with calibrated prediction intervals: a forecast that says how sure it is. Two interfaces over one engine, a one-second calculator and a fourteen-view analysis console. XGBoost, at a hold-out R² of 0.856. Not public yet, and the curve above is a schematic, not its data.<br>
<sub><i>Python, FastAPI, scikit-learn, XGBoost · Next.js</i></sub>
